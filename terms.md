# Terms of use

These terms apply to the MeshLive app for iPhone and Mac. Using the app means you agree to them.

## Who can use MeshLive
You must be 18 or older.

## What MeshLive is
MeshLive connects devices that are near each other, directly over Bluetooth. There is no account and no MeshLive server, and the
MeshLive team cannot see your calls or livestreams.

## Your responsibility
What you say, show or send is your responsibility. Do not use MeshLive to harass, threaten or stalk anyone, to spread hate, to
share sexual content involving minors, to do anything illegal, to impersonate someone, to spam people nearby, or to interfere
with other people's devices. Respect other people's privacy: do not record or stream someone who has not agreed to it where the
law or good manners require their agreement.

## Zero tolerance for abuse
We have zero tolerance for abusive content and behaviour. If you see it, block or report the person.

## Block and report
- Block: from an incoming call, a livestream, Recent, a contact or the list of people nearby, Block hides the person on your device and stops their calls.
- Report: Report also blocks them and prepares an email to meshlive.dev@gmail.com that you read and send yourself. Nothing is
  sent for you.
- Because MeshLive has no server, the team cannot remove a livestream, close an account or ban a device. We read reports and use
  them to improve blocking and safety features, and we answer when you write to us.
  If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services.

## Not for emergencies
MeshLive does not replace calling emergency services. Calls and livestreams work only with people within Bluetooth range and
only while both devices run MeshLive.

## Calls and livestreams
Calls are end-to-end encrypted between the two devices. Livestreams are public: anyone nearby can join one.
