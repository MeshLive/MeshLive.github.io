# Child safety standards

Last updated: 22 October 2026

MeshLive has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material
(CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any sexualisation of minors. Using MeshLive for any of this is forbidden.

## How MeshLive works, and what that means

MeshLive connects phones directly over Bluetooth. There are no MeshLive servers: calls, chats and livestreams go from
phone to phone and are end-to-end encrypted. We never receive, store or see this content, so we cannot scan or delete
it on anyone's phone. Our safety measures work within that design.

## What you can do in the app

- **Block** anyone: their calls, messages and livestreams no longer reach you.
- **Report** someone: they are blocked and everything from them is hidden on your phone. Then tap **Tell MeshLive** to
  email us about it.
- **Contact us** at any time from **Settings > Contact us**.
- Livestreams are public, so never share private information or images in one.

## How to report a concern

Email **[meshlive.dev@gmail.com](mailto:meshlive.dev@gmail.com)**, or use **Settings > Contact us** or **Report > Tell
MeshLive** in the app. Please describe what happened, when and where.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency number first. You can also report child
sexual abuse material to [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/) or your country's hotline listed by
[INHOPE](https://www.inhope.org/EN/articles/report-child-sexual-abuse-material).

## What we do

- We review every report we receive.
- When we gain actual knowledge of CSAM, we report it to NCMEC and the relevant authorities, as the law requires, and
  we cooperate with law enforcement requests that comply with applicable law.
- We improve the app's safety features (blocking, reporting, hiding) based on reports.
- We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations.

MeshLive is not intended for children under 13.
