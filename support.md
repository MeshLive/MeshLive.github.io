# Support

- Something does not work: email meshlive.dev@gmail.com with your device model, system version (Android, iOS or macOS) and what happened. In the iPhone and Mac app,
  Settings > Advanced > Help and feedback > Export diagnostics shows a short technical text you can add; it has no names, IDs,
  messages or media.
- Report a person or a livestream: use Block or Report in the call, on the livestream or in the list of people nearby.
- How to use MeshLive: the user guide at https://meshlive.github.io/ explains calls, adding contacts and watching livestreams.
- Need your data gone: Settings > Delete everything (iPhone and Mac).
- On Android, Settings > Contact us opens an email to the same address.
- Security problems: write to the same address and say "security" in the subject.
