Video calls and livestreams, without the internet.

MeshLive connects phones and laptops that are near each other, over Bluetooth. Every phone running it passes calls and streams along, so the more people use it, the further it reaches.

No internet, no SIM card, no account. Free.

Also on Windows. Coming soon to the Mac.

How it works

Calls and livestreams
Voice and video calls with people nearby. Go live with your camera, or with just your voice, like a local radio that keeps playing with the screen off.
No internet needed
Devices talk to each other directly over Bluetooth, up to about 10 to 30 metres apart.
Every phone passes it on
A livestream hops from phone to phone, so it reaches further with every person nearby who has MeshLive.
Private by design
Calls are end-to-end encrypted. There are no accounts, ads or servers.

Made for places without signal or Wi-Fi: hikes, festivals, trips, basements and emergencies.

Privacy and safety

Strangers nearby only see a random name that changes every 15 minutes. People you call, and people who watch your livestream, can see it is you. You can block anyone, and delete everything from your device in seconds.

MeshLive has not had an independent security audit yet, so do not rely on it when your safety depends on it.

Read the privacy policy and our child safety standards.
