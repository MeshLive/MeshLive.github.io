MeshLive

LIVE, WITHOUT THE INTERNET

Calls, chats and livestreams over a Bluetooth mesh. Every phone passes it on, so you reach further than Bluetooth alone. No internet, no SIM, no account.

Download for Android Get it on Obtainium Source code

Free and open source · beta

What it does

Calls

  • Voice and video calls with people nearby
  • End-to-end encrypted, phone to phone
  • Missed calls, call back, block unknown callers

Livestream

  • Go live and share your camera with everyone around you
  • Audio only, like a local radio that keeps playing with the screen off
  • Phones in between pass your stream along

Contacts

  • Add people by scanning each other's code
  • Call them whenever they are nearby
  • Strangers only see a random name that changes every 15 minutes

Your privacy

  • No internet, SIM, account or server
  • Your calls never touch the internet
  • Delete everything from the phone in seconds

Made for places with no signal or Wi-Fi: hikes, festivals, trips, basements, and emergencies.

How it works

Bluetooth on its own reaches about 10 to 30 metres. MeshLive turns every phone running it into a relay: your livestream or call hops from phone to phone, so the more people around you use MeshLive, the further you reach. Each hop adds a little delay and Bluetooth is slow, so video is small; audio-only streams reach the furthest.

Apps and status

Please read before relying on it. MeshLive is beta software and has not had an independent security review. On Android the ID a phone broadcasts is random and changes every 15 minutes (each app's privacy page says what it broadcasts), but a live stream shows its source to everyone who watches, and anyone whose call you accept learns who you are. Use it for testing and feedback, not where your safety depends on it.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no servers. Calls and streams are encrypted, and your real name is only sent, encrypted, to the people you choose. Read the full privacy policy, or the technical detail in the privacy document. Child safety: see the child safety standards.
